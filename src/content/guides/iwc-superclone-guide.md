## What is an IWC Superclone?

An IWC superclone is a replica watch built to 1:1 specifications with the genuine model. It uses:

- **Swiss ETA clone movement** (2824, 2892, or 7750 depending on the model)
- **904L stainless steel** — the same grade used by Rolex and genuine IWC
- **Full sapphire crystal** with anti-reflective coating
- **Hand-finished case and bracelet** with correct brushing and polishing
- **Correct weight** — 170-200g, matching the genuine
- **Working complications** — chronograph, date, power reserve as applicable

The key difference from a regular replica is precision. A $250 replica looks convincing from a distance. A $700 superclone is nearly indistinguishable in person.

## What Makes a Superclone "Super"?

Four things separate a superclone from a regular replica:

### 1. The Movement

A superclone uses a Swiss ETA clone (2824, 2892, or 7750). These movements are accurate to ±5 seconds per day, reliable, and serviceable by any local watchmaker. Regular replicas use Asian movements (DG2813, Miyota) that are accurate to ±10-20 seconds per day and often cannot be serviced.

### 2. The Steel

Superclones use 904L stainless steel — the same grade used by Rolex and genuine IWC. It is harder, brighter, and more corrosion-resistant than the 316L steel used in regular replicas. The difference is visible in the polish and felt in the weight.

### 3. The Crystal

Superclones use full sapphire crystal with anti-reflective coating. Regular replicas use sapphire-coated mineral glass, which scratches more easily and has a different light reflection.

### 4. The Finishing

Superclone cases and bracelets are hand-finished. The brushing is consistent, the edges are sharp, and the polishing is mirror-like. Regular replicas have machine-finished cases with soft edges and inconsistent brushing.

## How to Verify a Superclone Before Buying

Before you spend $500-$900, ask the seller for:

1. **QC photos** — Real photos of the actual watch, not stock images
2. **Running video** — A short video showing the second hand sweeping smoothly
3. **Timegrapher reading** — A photo of the watch on a timegrapher showing accuracy
4. **Serial number close-up** — To check engraving quality
5. **Movement photo** — If the case back is transparent, ask for a photo of the movement

A trusted seller will provide all five without hesitation. A scammer will make excuses.

## Care and Maintenance

A superclone is a mechanical watch and needs the same care as a genuine:

- **Service every 3-5 years** — $50-$100 at a local watchmaker
- **Avoid water** unless the watch is rated 5 ATM or higher
- **Store in a watch box** — Prevents scratches and dust
- **Wind gently** — Over-winding damages the mainspring
- **Keep away from magnets** — Magnets affect accuracy
- **Clean with a soft cloth** — Avoid abrasive materials
